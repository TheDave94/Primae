## What was built (`PrimaeNative/Core/Sonification/Candidates/WorldRT/`, not wired into the study path)
- **Port of WORLD** (`mmorise/World`, modified-BSD, notice kept in `WorldCommon.swift`):
  - CheapTrick: F0-adaptive 3-period Hann, DC correction, 2f0/3 linear smoothing, q1 = −0.15 liftering.
  - D4C with LoveTrain (threshold 0.85, 3 kHz bands).
  - Constants pinned in the tests from the source at the reference commit, not from memory.
- **Analysis (all in init):**
  - F0 comes from OUR pitch marks (`TDPSOLAAnalysis`), as a 5-mark smoothed period contour on 5 ms frames.
  - Per-mark jitter (clamped ±0.1) and shimmer (clamped to [0.5, 2]).
- **Live synthesis:** min-phase pulse-at-a-time inside `render` (vDSP N=2048): periodic part plus WORLD's seeded noise
  filtered by aperiodicity, DC remover, overlap-add into a ring; read-head in parameter time at the rate; pitch via
  pf = 2^(c/1200) on the pulse interval.
- **Behaviour:** loop wraps in the parameter domain (envelope crossfade ≤ 100 ms); the recorded attack, identical to
  shipped TDPSOLA, hands over to the vocoder over 2 periods; F uses candidate A's band-limited resample, rate ignored
  (U1); the carrier delegates to shipped TDPSOLA.
- **Flags (`WorldRTTuning`, defaults):** `jitterCarry` 0.8 / `shimmerCarry` 0.5; `gamma` 0 (formant warp when > 0);
  `levelCalibration` on (0 c = source RMS); `pitchLevelCompensation` on (P4, ±6 dB); `vocodedAttack` off;
  `seamSeconds` 0.1; `handoverPeriods` 2; `smoothingSeconds` 0.002.

- **Oracle:** `C2-oracle/oracle.cpp` (WORLD C++ built from source, sha256s in `C2-oracle/README.md`) gives the pinned spectral and aperiodicity values.

2. **The A F0 instrument is weak on breathy A** (TDPSOLA itself reads up to 4.2 c). The A output-ACF check is not gated; the pulse-train check is.
3. **LoveTrain rejected voiced frames inside the marked span:** I 6, L 2, A 0, M 0. If those frames fall in the loop, they render as noise bursts. Not heard in the WAVs; unmeasured on the iPad.
4. **`pitchLevelCompensation` (P4) is on by default.** That is David's call; the `WorldRT-nolevelcomp` entry exists for comparison.
5. **The recorded attack is unpitched**, matching shipped behaviour. The `vocodedAttack` flag exists.
6. **Licence:** a shipped binary must carry WORLD's BSD notice in the app's documentation/about screen.
7. **Init cost:** about 0.6 s per voiced letter on the Mac (-O; the iPad was not timed separately) and about 4 MB per renderer.
8. **Re-trigger noise:** see the M2 survivor above; unpinned.
