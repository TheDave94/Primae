## What the bench says, per question
- **Envelope held at ±600 (the original complaint):** no candidate meets 1.2 × Praat everywhere. TDPSOLA2 has the
  lowest mean LSD (1.92 dB) and most passes (41/60); the WORLD family has the lowest worst case (4.1–4.2 dB) but passes
  only 29/60, failing on A and M. γ 0.3 (both families) moves α as designed and fails the γ-0 LSD gate by construction.
- **Level:** only the WORLD family (WorldRT, PitchBank) is flat (≤ 0.15 dB); TDPSOLA2 halves ours' −600 drop.
- **F (unvoiced):** every candidate fixes it (item 1, shared). ours fails all three F gates.
- **Micro-variation:** TDPSOLA2 family passes almost everything; the WORLD family has a jitter tail (13–14 cells).
- **Control:** every C1 entry passes the step and lag gates; WorldRT misses trajectory lag once (I height trajectory,
  12.5 ms against 10); PitchBank misses step90 on 4 cells (≤ 16.3 ms).
- **Attack:** only item 7 moves it; PitchBank (bank path) is closest on A, TDPSOLA2-sa on I/L/M; none passes A.
- **Onset:** PitchBank adds 18.5 ms on I (PitchBank-int 38.5 ms) and 1.8–2.5 ms on A/M; the C1 and C2 entries are
  within 1.5 ms of ours on every letter.
