## What C3 is (spec: BAKEOFF-PITCH.md §2 C3)
- 13 banks over ±600 c at 100 c steps, rendered off the render thread by `WorldBankRenderer` (C2's analysis and
  synthesis offline, index-preserving layouts), cached by a two-entry `PitchBankCache` (active letter + one).
- Live: TDPSOLA2's voiced PSOLA reads bank round(cents/100) with a residual of at most ±50 c. A bank change is a
  mark-aligned equal-power crossfade over two periods (`PitchBank-lin`: linear). The noise swap reads the banks' own
  aperiodic parts. Item 7 is on: the attack plays at the steady F0 × pf (`PitchBank-int`: own intonation × pf).
- Banks not ready at gate-open → C1 live; `lastPath` reports `.live` / `.bank`. The export field is undecided.
- In the bench the banks are waited for at init (`waitForBanks`), so the bench measures the bank path only.
