## What C1 is (spec: BAKEOFF-PITCH.md §2 C1)
`TDPSOLA2Options`; `.r27` reproduces shipped TDPSOLA bit for bit (RELAYED: `TDPSOLA2IdentityTests`).
1. `unvoicedResample`: F is a band-limited resample of the unvoiced loop (r28 candidate A, `5dcc4b4f`).
2. `voicedWindowCap`: synthesis half-width capped on downshift (r28 candidate B, `a601f528`, generalised).
3. `pulseDensityExponent` 0.5: level gain pf^-0.5 below pf 1.
4. `harmonicNoiseSwap`: the aperiodic part of a grain is read from a nearby mark (seeded, deterministic).
5. `gciSnap`: analysis marks snap to SEDREAMS-style GCIs, with a jitter guard and a phase-aligned fallback.
6. `formantGamma`: partial formant co-shift (0 = held; `TDPSOLA2-g0.3` = 0.3).
7. `shiftedAttack` (added by lane C3, off by default): the voiced attack is PSOLA'd from its first full period at the
   steady F0 × pf (`TDPSOLA2-sa`), or with its own intonation × pf (`TDPSOLA2-sa-int`).
Bench entries: `TDPSOLA2` (items 1–5, γ 0), `TDPSOLA2-g0.3`, `TDPSOLA2-sa`, `TDPSOLA2-sa-int`, and the leave-one-out
entries `TDPSOLA2-no1..no5` (not re-run here; see Ablation).
