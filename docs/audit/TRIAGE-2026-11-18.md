## 3. Ranked shortlist of genuinely OPEN items

Ranked by study value first, then cost. "Protocol-touching" = changes what the child experiences
or what/how a row records → needs a ruling, not a lane.

1. **R-037 — sound-arm level integrity (MEASURED).** F's phoneme file needs **3.00x** gain
   (`loudnessTargetRMS 0.1463 / file RMS 0.0487`), A/I/L/M 1.86-1.90, applied at
   `AudioEngine.swift:302` via `player.volume`, whose documented range is 0-1. If iOS clamps above
   1.0, F plays up to 3x quieter than its arm-mates in the phoneme arm — a per-letter level
   confound inside the manipulation.
   *Change:* one line — apply the gain in the sonification kernel (already does per-source gain)
   instead of `player.volume`, or re-bake F to the target RMS. **Needs a ruling** (AE is
   DO-NOT-MODIFY, D7); measurement M6 is Mac-only and decides it first.
2. **R-014 — scored tolerance scales with sqrt(glyph area).** `TracingViewModel.swift:222, 535,
   3917`. I's radius is ~2.5-3x tighter than M's, which drives guided checkpoints, the sound gate
   and `formAccuracy = 3r` — a letter-difficulty confound inside the subset design.
   *Change:* height-based radius (`0.1 * gr.height`). **Protocol-touching (E+R).**
3. **R-072 — a fade-out can wipe a freshly loaded file.** `AudioEngine.swift:440-452` ends in
   `finishStop()` (`:480` `currentFile = nil`); `loadAudioFile` (`:295-302`) does not cancel
   `fadeOutTask`, so a letter/phase load inside a 0.12-0.4 s fade is silently dropped → a silent
   stroke or a silent observe.
   *Change:* capture a generation token in `stop()`'s task and drop the tail when `currentFile`
   changed, or cancel `fadeOutTask` at the top of `loadAudioFile`. **Needs a ruling** (AE, D7).
4. **R-091 — VoiceOver/Switch users cannot trace at all.** grep `accessibilityDirectTouch` = 0;
   the canvas is a raw touch surface.
   *Change:* one modifier on the tracing surface — `.accessibilityDirectTouch(true)` (plus an
   announcement on the retry cue). **Lane-ready, not protocol-touching (rev N).**
5. **R-104 — 37 AudioEngineTests never execute in CI.** `Primae.xcscheme:87-90` skips the whole
   suite (and ~32 methods again individually), so every audio regression is invisible to CI.
   *Change:* delete the suite-level skip, keep the per-method skips that have a reason, and add a
   test-count floor. **Lane-ready, not protocol-touching.**
